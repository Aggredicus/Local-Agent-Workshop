# Human Approval Boundaries

Human approval is required before:

- protected branch merges,
- production deployments,
- live credential use,
- payment live effects,
- destructive actions,
- customer data access,
- secret rotation,
- irreversible migrations,
- public releases,
- or high-risk architecture changes.

Agents may prepare drafts, tests, simulations, and reviewable patches, but may not activate sensitive consequences without explicit approval.
